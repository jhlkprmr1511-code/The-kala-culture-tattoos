QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

😣

How painful is the tattooing process?

Pain levels vary depending on the tattoo's size, placement, and your personal tolerance. Our professional artists work carefully to make the experience as comfortable as possible.

💰

How much does a tattoo cost?

Tattoo pricing depends on the size, design, placement, and detailing required. Our starting price is ₹399 per sq.inch, and special offers may be available. Contact us for an exact quote.

🔄

Can you cover up my old tattoo?

Yes! We offer custom tattoo cover-ups. Our artist will assess your existing tattoo and create a design that effectively incorporates or conceals it.

🧴

How do I care for my new tattoo?

Follow the aftercare instructions provided by our artist. Keep the tattoo clean, avoid unnecessary touching or scratching, and follow the recommended moisturizing and healing routine.

📅

Do I need to book an appointment?

Appointments are recommended, especially for larger or customized tattoos. Contact The Kala Culture Tattoos on WhatsApp or call us to discuss your idea and reserve your slot.

🎨

How do I choose the right design?

You don't have to decide everything yourself. Our artist can discuss your idea, preferred style, size, and placement with you and help create a custom design that suits your vision.
