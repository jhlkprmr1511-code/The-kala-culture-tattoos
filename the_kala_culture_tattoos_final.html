Frequently Asked Questions

Tattoo FAQs

Quick answers before you book your tattoo in Vadodara.

How painful is the tattooing process?

Pain varies by placement and individual tolerance. Our artists explain the process and help you choose a suitable placement and size.

How much does a tattoo cost at The Kala Culture Tattoos?

Tattoo pricing depends on size, placement, style and detail. Contact us for a personalised quote.

Can you cover up my old or unwanted tattoo?

Yes. We can assess your existing tattoo and discuss suitable cover-up or rework options based on the original tattoo.

How do I properly care for my new tattoo?

Follow the aftercare instructions given by the studio, keep the tattoo clean, avoid unnecessary sun exposure and follow the recommended healing routine.

Do I need an appointment before getting a tattoo?

Appointments are recommended so the artist can discuss your idea, placement, size and design before your session.

Are your tattooing practices hygienic?

The Kala Culture Tattoos follows strict hygiene practices, including sterilised equipment and disposable needles for tattooing.
